Privacy Policy

Updated 26 September 2026

The short version: no accounts, your game data stays on your device, and ads are provided by Google — you choose whether they're personalised.

Who we are

Games are created by Huurre Studio. Contact: hello@huurrestudio.com.

Your game data

Your player name, scores and settings are stored only on your device. We never receive them. Deleting the app deletes them.

Ads

Deep Signal is free and shows ads from Google AdMob between rounds. To show and measure ads, Google may process your IP address, device information, ad interactions and — only if you allow tracking — your advertising identifier. See how Google uses this data.

Your choices

  • Change ad consent any time in the app: Settings → Privacy Choices (where available).
  • iPhone and iPad: turn tracking off in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android: delete or reset your advertising ID in Settings → Google → Ads (on some phones Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Ask us about your data any time, or contact your data protection authority.

In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, personalised ads are based on your consent; basic ads and fraud prevention on legitimate interest.

Children

Games are not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If anything changes, we'll update this page.
