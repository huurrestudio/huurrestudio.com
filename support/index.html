Deep Signal

How do I play?

Drag the control wheel to steer. Your ship fires by itself.

Can I play my own music?

Yes: Settings → Background Music → Your Music, then play music from any app.

How do I change my name?

Settings → Change Name.

How do I change my ad choices?

In the app: Settings → Privacy Choices. On iPhone: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. On Android: Settings → Google → Ads.

Goat Bash: Fjord Trolls

How do I play?

Press anywhere, drag back and let go: the selected goat leaps the opposite way. Tap while it's in the air for its special move. Knock every troll into the water to clear the level.

Why can't I press Go?

Before a level you pick your herd: every leap needs a goat. Tap the goats (or +) until all leaps are filled, then press Go!

What do the goats do?

Pip leaps the farthest and dashes to wherever you tap. Bramble's bleat blows trolls away. Big Billy hits the hardest and slams down with a shockwave.

What about ads?

Goat Bash shows family-friendly ads between levels, and you can choose to watch a short video for 2 extra leaps. Ads are never personalised and the game doesn't use your advertising ID. Playing offline means no ads. More in the Goat Bash privacy policy.
