Help & questions

How do I throw?

Put your finger on the disc at the bottom of the screen, pull it down for power and let go. Slide sideways while pulling to tilt the disc: it curves the way it's tilted. Drag anywhere else to look around.

Which disc should I use?

Drivers fly farthest but need a strong arm, putters are the most accurate. The number under each disc is how far it goes for you. Your arm gets stronger as you level up.

The game runs slowly on my phone

Open Settings → Graphics and choose Performance. It applies right away.

How do I change my ad choices?

In the game: Settings → Privacy Choices (where available). On iPhone: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. On Android: Settings → Google → Ads. See the privacy policy.
